# Japanese Language School (Nihongo Gakko) Compliance Skill

This skill ensures that all copy, forms, and administrative tools match Yapon tili maktablari (Japanese language school) terminology and official regulations under Japanese Immigration (Nyukan).

## Terminology Reference

1. **Nyukan (入国管理局 - 入管):** Immigration Services Agency of Japan.
2. **CoE (在留資格認定証明書):** Certificate of Eligibility. This is the official form generated to bring foreign students to Japan.
3. **Zairyu Card (在留カード):** Resident card issued to foreigners residing in Japan for mid-to-long term. Must check visa expiry dates closely.
4. **Sekigae (席替え):** Seating chart rearrangement. Critical for classroom management and preventing native language clusters (e.g., student seating balancing).
5. **Shindansho (診断書):** Doctor's diagnostic certificate/medical slip. Excused absences must have this logged to override visa penalty.

## System Auditing Rules

* Ensure EJU and JLPT mock exams are logged accurately.
* Guard the 28-hour weekly work rule (Arubaito) strictly.
